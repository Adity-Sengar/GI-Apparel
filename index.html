Return & Exchange Policy

Please review our exchange terms before placing your order.

At GI Apparel, we want you to love every piece you receive. As our products are carefully made and packed for you, please read the following policy before placing your order.

1. Exchange Window

We accept exchange requests within 7 days from the date of delivery.

The product must be unused, unworn, unwashed and unaltered, with all original tags and packaging intact.

2. Eligible for Exchange

An exchange may be requested for:

  • Size-related issues
  • Wrong product received
  • Damaged or defective product received

For a wrong, damaged or defective product, please contact GI Apparel within 48 hours of delivery and share clear photos/videos of the product and packaging.

3. Size Exchange

We offer one size exchange per order, subject to availability.

For a size exchange, the customer is responsible for the reverse shipping/pickup charge. GI Apparel will ship the replacement size without charging an additional forward-shipping fee.

4. Wrong, Damaged or Defective Product

If you receive a wrong, damaged or defective product, GI Apparel will arrange the exchange and cover the applicable return and replacement shipping costs, subject to verification.

5. Non-Eligible Products

Exchange requests will not be accepted if:

  • The product has been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery
  • Original tags are missing or removed
  • Original packaging is not available where applicable
  • The exchange request is made after the 7-day exchange window
  • The product is customised or personalised, where applicable

6. Exchange Process

To request an exchange, please contact GI Apparel through our official WhatsApp or email within the applicable exchange period. Please provide your order number and the reason for the exchange. Photos/videos may be requested for verification.

Once the request is approved, our team will share the next steps for the return/pickup and replacement.

7. Refunds

GI Apparel currently follows an exchange-only policy for eligible orders. Refunds are not available for change of mind, size preference or other personal preference. If an ordered product is unavailable for an approved exchange due to an issue caused by GI Apparel, our team will provide an appropriate resolution.

8. Important Notes

  • Please check your order as soon as it is delivered.
  • We recommend recording an unboxing video when opening your parcel, especially for any damage or wrong-product claim.
  • Exchange approval is subject to the product meeting the eligibility conditions above.
  • Customers are requested to retain the original packaging until the exchange is completed.

9. Contact Us

For exchange requests or questions regarding this policy, please contact GI Apparel through the contact details provided on our website.

Policy Summary

  • 7-day exchange window
  • One size exchange per order
  • Customer pays reverse shipping for size exchange
  • GI Apparel covers shipping for wrong/damaged/defective products
  • Exchange-only policy
